Privacy Policy for "Cash Box"

Last updated: 2026-10-30

Data the app stores

The app stores the denomination counts you enter, your count history, your chosen currency and settings, and the name you type in Settings. All of it is saved only on your device.

Data sharing

The app does not collect, send, or share any data with us or any third party. It works without an internet connection and contains no ads, analytics, or tracking.

When you use "Export backup" or "Export history", you choose where the file goes. That file is then handled by the app or service you pick.

Deleting your data

You can delete all data from Settings inside the app, or by uninstalling the app.

Children

The app is a business tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
